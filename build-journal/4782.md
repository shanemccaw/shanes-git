## Design decisions worth knowing

- Postgres channels are namespaced `sg:<name>`. The DB is shared with unrelated code that may use NOTIFY; without the prefix a subscriber could read those. Channel names: 1–60 chars `A-Za-z0-9_.:-`, case-sensitive. psql form: `NOTIFY "sg:<channel>", 'payload';`
- `publish()` throws over 7999 bytes rather than letting Postgres fail/truncate (the 8000-byte NOTIFY cap, per the #4782 comment). Invalidation-only payloads are the intended pattern.
- NOTIFY is not durable. Anything published while the listener is down is lost; subscribers get `resync` on reconnect and must re-fetch. The transport cannot replay.
- Added beyond the literal scope (small, security-relevant for a long-lived stream): revoked tokens close their open streams (`isTokenLive` in tokens.ts, no `call_count` bump).
